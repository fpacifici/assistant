# Garden plan 2026

## Vegetables

Tomatoes this year: San Marzano for sauce and Black Krim for salads. Sow indoors in early March, plant out after 10 May when nights stay above 10°C.

- Courgettes: two plants are plenty
- Basil between the tomatoes
- Lettuce in the shade bed, sow every three weeks

## Fruit

The fig tree needs pruning in late February. Net the strawberries in May against the blackbirds.

## Watering

The drip timer runs at 6:30 for 20 minutes from June to September.
