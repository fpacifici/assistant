# Grandmother's lasagna

Serves six. The secret is the slow ragù and a thin béchamel.

## Ragù

Soffritto of onion, carrot and celery, then 500 g of minced beef and pork, half a glass of red wine and 700 g of passata. Simmer for at least three hours on the lowest heat.

## Béchamel

50 g butter, 50 g flour, 1 litre of whole milk, nutmeg and salt. Keep it runny: it thickens in the oven.

## Assembly

Six layers of fresh egg pasta, ragù, béchamel and grated Parmigiano. Finish with béchamel and Parmigiano on top.

Bake at 190°C for 40 minutes, then let it rest for 15 minutes before cutting.
