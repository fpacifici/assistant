# Japan itinerary, November 2026

Two weeks, 3 to 17 November 2026. Flights Milan to Tokyo Haneda with a stop in Helsinki.

## Plan

- 3-7 Nov: Tokyo, hotel near Shinjuku station
- 7-11 Nov: Kyoto, ryokan Yoshida-sanso near Ginkakuji, two nights with kaiseki dinner
- 11-13 Nov: Nara and Osaka day trips from Kyoto
- 13-17 Nov: back to Tokyo, day trip to Nikko

## Rail pass

The 14-day JR Pass costs 80,000 yen per person. It covers the Shinkansen Tokyo to Kyoto except the Nozomi trains. Buy it online before leaving.
