# Packing checklist

Use for every trip longer than three days.

## Documents

- Passport and a photocopy in the other bag
- Travel insurance card (policy number in the Passport and visas note)
- Driving licence if renting a car

## Electronics

- Phone charger and power bank
- Universal plug adapter (type A for Japan and the US, type C for Europe)
- Noise cancelling headphones

## Health

- Antihistamines and plasters
- Never pack amoxicillin or other penicillins: allergic, see the health note
