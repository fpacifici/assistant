# Lisbon trip, April 2025

We flew to Lisbon on 12 April 2025 and came back on 19 April.

## Where we stayed

Hotel Casa das Janelas Verdes in Lapa, room 204 with a view over the river. Breakfast was included and the staff booked our tram tickets.

## Food

- Pastéis de nata at Manteigaria in Chiado, best ones of the trip
- Grilled sardines at Taberna do Mar on the second night
- Dinner at Cervejaria Ramiro: the garlic prawns are a must, book a table or queue for an hour

## Day trips

Sintra by train from Rossio station, 40 minutes. Pena Palace tickets must be bought online for a time slot. We skipped Cascais because of the rain.

## Next time

Stay in Alfama instead of Lapa: Lapa was quiet but far from the tram 28 stops.
