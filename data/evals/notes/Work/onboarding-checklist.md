# Onboarding checklist for new engineers

## Before day one

- Open an IT ticket for the laptop at least ten working days before the start date
- Create the accounts: email, GitHub, Slack, the on-call tool
- Pick an onboarding buddy from another team

## First week

- Join the Slack channels #eng, #incidents and #heron
- Pair with the buddy on a small production change and ship it
- Read the last three postmortems

## First month

- Shadow one on-call shift
- One-to-one with the engineering manager every week
