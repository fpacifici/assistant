# Project Heron kickoff

Meeting on 2 February 2026. Attendees: Giulia (product), Tomás (backend), Aiko (mobile), me.

## Goal

Heron replaces the customer self-service portal with a mobile-first app, starting with invoices and payment methods.

## Decisions

- Public launch on 15 September 2026, with a closed beta for 200 customers in June
- Aiko owns the mobile app, Tomás owns the billing API, Giulia owns the beta programme
- We reuse the existing authentication service, no new login flow
- Weekly demo every Thursday at 16:00
